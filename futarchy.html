Information, not instruction. Conditional markets forecast what the measure might look like under each outcome. They inform the DAO's debate; the vote remains with token holders.

Conditional design Same measure, two worlds

Both panels resolve against the identical success measure and target, so the difference between them isolates the proposal's expected effect.

Reading the gap Small gap, small edge

A wide positive gap says traders expect the proposal to help. A narrow gap says the market sees little difference between the two futures.

Decision layer The vote stays human

Futarchy here produces a forecast, not an outcome. Governance keeps the final call and answers to its own criteria.
