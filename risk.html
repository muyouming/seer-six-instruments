Bands are read from the estimates on this page, not assigned by hand. Illustrative concept data — no live markets are connected.
Why compare A single price has no context

One yes-or-no market tells you a number. Twelve in one frame tell you where the market thinks risk concentrates — and whether a protocol's estimate is an outlier in its category.

What the number is Market-implied PD, 12 months

Each estimate is priced continuously by traders. It is a forecast, not a rating, and it re-estimates every quarter against the same event definition.

Reading habit Compare, then dig

Rank by band, check the quarter change, and only then open the underlying market. The table is the entry point, not the answer.
