Which submissions are likely to be selected?

Traders price the field: each entry on a board like this carries a market-implied probability of being selected. It is a signal reviewers can weigh — the final selection stays with them.

What a price is An expectation, not an outcome

A price of 27% says traders think selection is roughly one-in-four likely. It never selects anything by itself.

Who decides Reviewers keep discretion

The panel reads the board the way it reads any other signal — one input among criteria, context and judgement. The decision column stays open until they act.

How to use it Read the board, not one row

Selection is relative. A rank and a gap between neighbours carry more information than any single probability quoted in isolation.
